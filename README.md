# Survey effort, climate change, and the discovery of new bird distribution records in China

Discrete-time survival analysis of **new provincial bird distribution records** across mainland
China and Hong Kong (2002–2024), separating the roles of **survey effort**, **accumulated
climate change** and **interannual climate variability** in generating a new record.

---

## Headline result

> **Survey effort dominates the discovery process, but accumulated climate change raises
> new-record hazard independently — and does so most strongly where survey effort is low.**

Final model (SDM threshold 50; annual mean temperature; 15-year accumulation window;
effort = observer visits):

```
event ~ clim_change_z * effort_z + clim_var_z + (1 | species) + (1 | province)
family = binomial("cloglog")        # discrete-time proportional hazards
```

| Term | HR per 1 SD | 95% CI | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survey effort | **1.788** | 1.639–1.951 | 7.7 × 10⁻³⁹ |
| Accumulated climate change (15 yr) | **1.394** | 1.244–1.562 | 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ |
| Annual climate variability | **0.850** | 0.781–0.926 | 1.8 × 10⁻⁴ |
| Climate change × effort | **0.876** | 0.801–0.959 | 3.9 × 10⁻³ |

Intercept −5.906 → baseline annual hazard **0.272 %**.
n = 182,485 species × province × year rows, 655 events, 394 species, 32 provincial units.

**Relative importance** (drop-term contribution to explained deviance, consistent across
three SDM thresholds): survey effort **78 %**, accumulated climate change **15 %**,
annual climate variability **7 %**, interaction **4 %**.

---

## Why the three-way climate decomposition matters

The species-specific climate gradient

```
x(s,p,t) = [T(p,t) − T_base(p)] − [N(s,t) − N_base(s)]
```

(province anomaly minus the anomaly over the species' Chinese historical range; baseline
1980–2000) is decomposed **using only backward-looking information**, as required in a
survival model:

| Component | Definition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `clim_change` | trailing 15-yr mean of `x` | accumulated climate change |
| `clim_var` | `x(t) − clim_change(t)` | interannual climate variability |
| `b_static` | `T_base(p) − N_base(s)` | historical climate mismatch (dropped; see report) |

Without this split the two components **cancel**: accumulated change acts positively,
interannual variability negatively, and an undecomposed annual anomaly shows no effect at all.
The signal only emerges at accumulation windows of **≥ 10 years** (at a 5-year window the
climate effect is HR = 1.054, *P* = 0.30), which is itself a mechanistic result: new records
respond to sustained warming, not to single warm years.

## Robustness

| Dimension | Result |
|---|---|
| SDM threshold (50 / 100 / 200) | climate change HR 1.397 / 1.411 / 1.406; interaction 0.873 / 0.875 / 0.874 |
| Effort proxy (visits, records, observers, birding-days, PCA) | climate change 1.371–1.408 (all *P* ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁸); interaction 0.839–0.895 (all *P* ≤ 0.033) |
| Accumulation window (5 / 10 / 15 / 20 yr) | ΔAIC vs effort-only −0.8 / −25.6 / **−42.1** / −40.3 |
| Climate indicator | annual mean T best; warmest-month Tmax intermediate; winter mean T and coldest-month Tmin show no signal under this decomposition |

**Model diagnostics (DHARMa, 500 simulations)** — KS uniformity *P* = 0.66; dispersion
*P* = 0.41; province-grouped residuals *P* = 0.96; temporal autocorrelation DW = 1.80,
*P* = 0.62; spatial autocorrelation Moran's I = −0.025, *P* = 0.79. Year- and species-grouped
residuals deviate (*P* = 0.031 and 0.0011), indicating residual species-level heterogeneity
that is reported as a limitation.

## Data sources

- **New-record events** — China Bird New Records compilation, 2002–2024, resolved to province and year.
- **Survey effort** — combined eBird/GBIF + China Bird Report province-year panel; four
  observation proxies plus a PCA composite. Missing mainland province-years are treated as
  **structural zeros** (the panel is a record aggregation with no zero rows), not as missing data.
- **Climate** — WorldClim 2.1 10-arcmin CRU-TS 4.09 downscaled monthly series
  (tmin/tmax 1980–2024, precipitation 2000–2024); baseline 1980–2000.
- **Species ranges** — BirdLife International range polygons clipped to China.
- **Future climate** — CMIP6 four-GCM ensemble (ACCESS-CM2, MPI-ESM1-2-HR, MIROC6, UKESM1-0-LL),
  SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5.
- **Base map** — GS(2019)1822 national boundary and South China Sea nine-dash line.

## Scope decisions

- **Vagrants excluded.** 69 species without a Chinese historical range have no definable
  native-range climate baseline; they are dropped rather than imputed
  (`data/list_excluded_vagrants.csv`). The remaining 394 species all hold Chinese ranges, so
  the events analysed represent **range expansion / newly detected populations**, not vagrancy.
- **Taiwan and Macau excluded** on survey-effort grounds: recorded effort density is ~0.13
  records km⁻² for Taiwan versus ~30 for Beijing, indicating a source-coverage gap rather than
  genuinely low effort. Hong Kong is **retained** — its low absolute counts reflect its small
  area (16.5 records km⁻², comparable to Beijing).

## Caveats

1. **Survey effort and calendar year are collinear** (*r* = 0.79). Controlling for a linear year
   term attenuates the effort coefficient to HR ≈ 1.19 while strengthening the interaction; the
   main model does not include a year term, and this trade-off is reported explicitly rather than
   resolved by fiat.
2. **Late-century projections are extrapolations.** By 2080 effort is projected 1.7 SD (SSP2-4.5)
   to 3.4 SD (SSP5-8.5) beyond the observed range. Scenario maps are illustrations of model
   behaviour, not forecasts.
3. **Precipitation is not in the final model.** Precipitation panels were built but showed no
   signal under the three-way decomposition.
4. **Machine-learning attribution differs from the mechanistic decomposition.** TreeSHAP assigns
   survey effort 48.5 % versus 78 % in the deviance decomposition, because the boosted-tree model
   has no random effects and climate features partly encode species and province identity.
